Jogak Memories sewn into patchwork

An Instagram feed flattens every moment into the same square. Jogak takes each memory out of its square, keeps only its color and texture as dyed cloth, cuts it to the weight of the memory, and joins the pieces back together by hand, the way a jogakbo is pieced from leftover scraps.
