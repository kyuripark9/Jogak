Build a patchwork Jogak · From your own photos

Each photo keeps only its main colors and texture and is dyed into a scrap of cloth, sized by the weight of the memory and laid out loose. The seams join only when you sew them.

Grouped by the day and place each photo was taken. A day spent in two places becomes two memories.

Screenshots carry no dates, so neighboring posts with similar colors count as one day. The same screenshots as the grid check work here.
